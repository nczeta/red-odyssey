RED ODYSSEY

Martian Terrain Explorer

A modular WebGIS frontend being developed for the NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026. This is an independent project, without implied NASA endorsement.

Mars Imagery, Elevation, Slope, Contour Lines and Geological Map show Jezero Crater in the Jezero_Crater_Projection coordinate system. Coordinates are in Martian metres. Elevation and Slope preserve exported QGIS colors. Contour Lines uses ELEV values in metres, with a vertical interval of 200 m. Geological Map combines a geology raster and two polygon datasets; select a unit to inspect its recorded attributes. No point POI dataset is connected. Routing requires additional verified datasets and services.
